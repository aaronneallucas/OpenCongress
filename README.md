Open Congress is a way of visualizing and discovering data about Congress, including votes and each of its members.
It pulls data from Congress.gov through its API and Senate.gov via xml files.
Each roll call vote gets its own webpage and visualization, designed to make it easy to see how each member of congress voted.
The main page has a directory of roll call votes and of members.
Each member gets a page, showing their sponsored and cosponsored legislation, and how often they vote against their party and how often they miss votes.
There is a directory of members to see which members miss the most votes or vote against their party the most often.
