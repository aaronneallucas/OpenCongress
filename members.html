Members

Missed votes: share of roll calls, while serving, where the member was recorded as Not Voting. Against party: share of the member's Yea/Nay votes that went against the majority of their own party on that roll call (ties, Present votes, and independents are not counted). Maverick score: on each roll call where a member voted against their party's majority, they earn the number of party members who voted with the majority minus the number who voted against, summed over all such votes. Sponsored and cosponsored show a dash until that member\u2019s bills have been collected (a few members are refreshed each run). Rank #1 means the most missed votes, the highest against-party share, or the highest Maverick score in that chamber and Congress. Missed and against-party ranks require at least a quarter of the chamber's maximum number of votes, so members who served briefly are not ranked. The data does not say why a vote was missed.

Data: Congress.gov and Senate.gov
