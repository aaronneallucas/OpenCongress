Bills, nominations and hearings

CongressBills collectedSummariesNominationsMeetings and hearingsDownloads

Bills are gathered from members’ sponsored and cosponsored lists, so “collected” can be below the Congress’s real total while it is being backfilled. Sponsors and cosponsors (with dates), laws and committee meetings fill in over several updates. A finished Congress shows final once its data can no longer change.

Per member files: data/legislation/<member id>.json. Member contact details: contact.json. Member list: directory.json.

Sources

  • Congress.gov and its API (Library of Congress): House roll call votes, members, sponsored and cosponsored bills, and laws.
  • Congress.gov API: also nominations (by Congress) and committee meetings and hearings.
  • congress-legislators: members’ official websites, phone numbers, contact forms and social accounts.
  • Senate.gov roll call votes: Senate votes, published as XML.

Statistics (missed votes, votes against party, Maverick score, rankings) are calculated by this site from those records. The methods are described at the bottom of the Members pages.
