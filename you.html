Rep. You

Mark how you would have voted on this Congress's roll calls and see which members vote most like you, as if you were a member of Congress.

When on, every vote page shows Yes / No buttons, and you appear as a black dot with a gold ring on your own voting map, reached from this page (it never appears on the main Relationships tab). You never appear in the member lists or vote lists. Your picks are saved only in this browser. Rep. You covers the current Congress only.
